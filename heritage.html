Living Heritage

The Sacred Tarakasi Technique

Tarakasi is an ancient Indian art form where ingots of pure 925 sterling silver are heated, drawn into hair-thin metallic wires through calibrated dies, and hand-twisted into delicate gossamer patterns.

Unlike mass-stamped or machine-cast silver, true Tarakasi requires steady hands, incredible patience, and decades of generational instinct. Each petal, scroll, and leaf in a Shree Ganga creation is bent and soldered individually over a pinpoint jewelers' flame.

Understanding Your 925 BIS Hallmark

Every piece sold by Shree Ganga Jewellers carries 3 distinct laser-stamped authentications.

01. BIS Logo

Bureau of Indian Standards

The official triangular insignia signifying certified compliance with national precious metal purity and testing regulations.

02. 925 Mark

Purity Grade Verification

Indicates exactly 92.5% pure elemental silver alloyed with 7.5% hardening minerals for eternal structural durability without bending.

03. Assayer Identification

Jeweler & Center Code

Unique assay mark identifying the licensed testing laboratory and Shree Ganga Jewellers' proprietary maker registry.
